6. Conservation Guidelines

6.1 Firefly Observation Ethics

When observing fireflies in the wild, all members must adhere to these principles:

  • Minimize light pollution by using red-filtered flashlights when necessary
  • Keep a respectful distance from fireflies and their habitats
  • Never capture fireflies unless part of an authorized research program
  • Stay on designated paths to avoid disturbing habitats
  • No flash photography that may disrupt mating patterns

6.2 Habitat Protection

Our members agree to support habitat protection by:

  • Advocating for the preservation of natural areas where fireflies live
  • Participating in habitat restoration projects when possible
  • Educating others about the importance of reducing light pollution
  • Encouraging sustainable practices that protect water quality in firefly habitats
